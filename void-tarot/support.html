Help with Void Tarot for iPhone

Need a person? Email Wild Honey on The Porch LLC. Replies usually arrive within 1–3 business days.

Email Void Tarot support

What to include in a bug report

  • your iPhone model and iOS version;
  • the Void Tarot version and build number;
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened; and
  • a screenshot or screen recording if the problem is visual.

Please avoid sending private questions, readings, birth details, or journal text unless that content is essential to reproduce the problem.

Frequently asked questions

Which devices are supported?

Void Tarot is a native iPhone app that requires iOS 26 or later.

Do I need an account?

No. Void Tarot has no sign-in and does not ask for an email address. The display name and birth information you choose to provide stay with your app data.

What is free?

Daily Void, Card Pull, Yes / No, Past / Present / Future, and Journal Ritual are free. The app includes 78 reviewed card meanings, journal entries, and access to past readings.

What does Premium include?

Premium opens Threshold, Pattern Break, Relation Mirror, Work / Money Current, The Abyss Cross, and Chakra Current.

What offer is available?

The app and Apple show the configured product, price, and any billing period before purchase. The offer may be a one-time unlock or an auto-renewing subscription. If you buy a subscription, it renews automatically until canceled through Apple.

How do I manage or cancel Premium?

Use the subscription-management option in Void Tarot Settings, or open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Follow Apple’s cancellation instructions. Canceling stops renewal; it does not erase readings or journals.

I paid, but Premium is still sealed.

Make sure the device is signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase, then choose Restore Purchases on the Premium screen. Allow a moment for Apple to return the verified purchase. If access still does not appear, email support with the purchase date and app version—never send payment card details.

What happens when Premium ends?

You cannot begin a new Premium reading without active access. Your past readings, journal entries, and five free readings remain available.

Does Void Tarot work offline?

The deck, free readings, deterministic meanings, journal, and locally saved history work without a network connection. App Store purchases and optional birthplace search require a connection. This build does not enable iCloud sync.

Where are voice notes stored?

A saved Journal voice memo stays on the device where it was recorded and is not uploaded by Void Tarot. If speech permission or on-device transcription is unavailable, you can continue without a transcript or without recording.

Does logging out delete my data?

No. Log out keeps your local profile, readings, journals, recordings, and preferences.

How do I delete my profile and data?

Open Settings in Void Tarot, choose Delete my profile and data, and confirm the destructive action. Deletion takes effect immediately and removes all local profiles and birth information, readings, journals and transcripts, voice recordings, and app preferences. It cannot be undone in the app.

Deleting your profile or the app does not cancel an Apple subscription or remove Apple purchase records. If you have a subscription, cancel it separately through Apple to stop renewal; see Apple’s cancellation instructions. You can delete local data immediately without waiting for a subscription to expire.

This does not erase existing device backups, exported copies, or any data synced by earlier builds. Manage those copies separately through their storage provider or Apple settings. This build does not enable iCloud sync. In-app deletion does not erase support emails; contact the privacy address below about correspondence.

What happens if I delete the app?

Deleting Void Tarot removes its local data from that iPhone. Purchases can be restored through Apple, but Restore Purchases does not recover deleted readings or journals. Backups and other copies are outside in-app deletion.

Does Void Tarot predict the future?

No. Void Tarot is for entertainment, reflection, and journaling. It does not claim psychic certainty and is not medical, legal, financial, mental-health, or crisis advice.
